# Red team, 28 October 2026: research, competitors, and the app itself

Scope:
- The research and competitor documents in `docs/`, checked against the web on 28 October 2026.
- Every screen of the app in three themes (light, dark, high contrast), both shipped languages (English, es-PE) and three widths (320, 390, 1280 px).
- The states that appear only after a tap: open forms, validation errors, galleries, the tour, Daily mode, running timers.
- Offline start.
- A shared device with two accounts.
- The four sync findings from the commercial red team (`docs/wiki/redteam-commercial-2026-10-28.md`, P0-2, P0-4, P1-7, P1-10).

Each finding gives what was wrong, why (the root cause), what changed, and the test that now holds it. Section 5 lists what was left alone and why.

## 1. Research and competitor documents

### R1. The app's own copy broke the project's copy policy
`docs/research-manual.md` section 11 asks for the exact source and no causal language for indirect evidence. About 45 strings in English and Spanish missed that bar. Examples:
- "Implementation intentions double follow-through, proven across 94 studies." Gollwitzer and Sheeran (2006) found a medium-to-large effect (d ≈ 0.65), not a doubling.
- "A missed day triggers full abandonment for 1 in 3 users." No source. Lally et al. (2010) found one missed day did not derail habit formation. The same line said skip tokens are "not [to] excuse laziness", which is shame language.
- "Pomodoro matches your brain's natural 90-minute ultradian cycle." This is weak in waking performance, and no ADHD trial of Pomodoro was found.
- "Starting the first step is 80% of the work", and "research-backed" labels with no linked source.

**Root cause:** copy was written from secondary summaries, and nothing checked it.

**Fix:** each string now says what the feature does, with a hedged reason where one is supported. After the merge with #89, the template "why" lines use #89's wording, which also drops the named-personality attributions.

**Test:** `src/i18n/claims.test.ts` fails if "research shows", "proven", "doubles", "science-backed" and similar return to the English or Spanish strings.

### R2. Competitor coverage had real holes
- **Tiimo** is Apple's iPhone App of the Year 2025 and now the reference ADHD planner.
  - Price is about $42 a year or $10 a month.
  - It went freemium in 2023 after users objected to a paywall.
  - Current store reviews complain that calendar import, widgets and repeats are paywalled.
- **Lunatask** was missing. It covers tasks, habits, mood and energy, and relationships, and has a free tier. "No competitor combines these" was too broad. Gylio's link to money is still unique.
- **AI planners were missing:** Motion, Akiflow, Amazing Marvin, Saner.ai. Sunsama is now about $16 a month annual.
- **Budget apps with free tiers were missing:** Goodbudget, EveryDollar, Rocket Money and Actual Budget. "Budget apps are $100 to $110 a year with no free tier" is true of YNAB and Monarch, not of the category.
- **Latin American apps were missing**, although Peru is the launch market: Mobills, Organizze, Fintonic, Wallet by BudgetBakers, Monefy, and the banks' own apps (BCP, Interbank, BBVA Peru). BBVA Peru reports that about 70% of Peruvians do not know how to make a family budget.

Sources are linked in `docs/competitor-gap-research.md`, which now carries these corrections.

### R3. Pricing documents disagree with each other and with the app
- `pricing-strategy-final.md` contradicts itself:
  - Its summary says Flow is "$4.99/mo annual", but its table says $4.08.
  - It requires "at least 35%" annual discount, but its table gives 32% and 26%.
- The "70% would switch from Todoist" figure is a reader poll of about 123 votes under an Android Authority article. It is not a customer survey. The Todoist price rise itself is real ($48 to $60 a year, 10 December 2025).
- Since #89, the pricing page sells Pro at US$6.99 a month, US$49.99 a year or S/ 14.90 with a 7-day trial. The upgrade prompt still says "$12/month · or $10/month billed yearly" (`upgrade.pricing`). The Accounts thread owns this (commercial red team P1-1), so it is not changed here.
- **Decision for the owner:** routines and rewards are paywalled. Paywalled basics are the main complaint that pushed Tiimo to freemium.

### R4. Evidence that is weaker or newer than the documents say
- **Body doubling:** there is still no controlled ADHD trial.
  - A 220-person survey (Eagle and Ringland, ACM TACCESS 2024) and a 12-person VR preprint (2025) are encouraging.
  - A small null result was presented in 2025.
  - Keep the feature optional and make no claim that it works.
- **Habit formation:** a 2024 meta-analysis (Singh et al., *Healthcare*, 20 studies) gives medians of 59 to 66 days, a range of 4 to 335, and only 23% reaching the threshold. "Repair, don't reset" is supported. A fixed-day promise is not.
- **`task-breakdown-research.md` problems:**
  - "2 to 3 times more dopamine stimulation" has no source.
  - "Interest-based nervous system" is a practitioner idea.
  - The "CHI 2024" section cites a March 2026 arXiv paper.
  - Miller's 7 ± 2 is now about 4 chunks (Cowan 2001).
- The app now says a step takes "a few minutes", not "under 10 minutes", to match the research document.

### R5. What this round could not do
- Read the primary papers in full (abstracts and summaries only).
- Mine store reviews at scale, or watch real people use the app. Competitor weaknesses remain hypotheses.
- Get Peru download rankings. Sensor Tower or Appfigures would settle it.

## 2. Every screen, every theme, both languages

Method: axe-core (WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A/AA plus best practice) ran on 8 routes × 3 combinations, then on the opened states. A second check flags any button whose spoken name leaves out its visible words (WCAG 2.5.3), and lists of Edit or Delete buttons a screen reader cannot tell apart. Both now run in `e2e/accessibility.spec.ts` on every CI run, and both report zero.

| # | What was wrong | Root cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Every task, event, plan and timer row had a button just called "Edit" or "Delete", so a screen reader heard a list of identical names. "Track time" was spoken as "Start tracking time on…", so a speech user saying what they see did nothing. | The checks looked at one empty screen at a time, never a list. | Each name now says which item, and contains the visible words: "Edit Water plants", "Track time on …", "Delete the stretch on …". |
| A2 | Error, warning and success text failed contrast in light mode: 3.5:1 for red, 2.1:1 for green, 2.0:1 for amber, against the 4.5:1 that text needs. | Fill tokens (`error`, `success`…) were used for text. They are background colours. | Text uses the `*Strong` tokens. `src/core/textColorTokens.test.ts` refuses a fill token as a text colour anywhere in `src`. |
| A3 | Primary and muted text dropped below 4.5:1 on the 8% tinted cards. | Contrast was checked on the plain background only. | Light primary is `#5250E8` and light muted `#625F77`; dark muted is `#9492A3`. `themes.test.ts` now checks both on every surface and its tint. |
| A4 | Daily mode added a second main landmark. Onboarding, pricing and sign-in had none. | Each screen chose its own root element. | One `main` per screen, and Daily mode is a region. |
| A5 | The task view tabs were ARIA tabs that ignored the arrow keys. | The tabs role was added without its keyboard pattern. | Roving tabindex with ←, →, Home and End (`src/core/utils/tabKeys.ts`). |

## 4. Sync, offline and the shared device

### S1 (P0-2). The old sync queue retried forever
- **Root cause:** the queue had no terminal rule. A 404 or 400 was retried with backoff for ever, and each update re-read and re-sorted the whole queue (O(N²)).
- **Fix:**
  - The server's permanent answers (400, 404, 405, 410, 413, 422) drop the change. It stays on the device and a console note says so.
  - `Retry-After` is honoured.
  - Each update is one IndexedDB transaction on one entry.
  - The queue also stays off whenever account sync is on, as before.
- **Tests:** `backgroundSync.queue.test.ts` and `offlineSync.test.ts`.

### S2 (P1-7). "Use server version" wiped a task's title
- **Root cause:** the Worker wraps the server's copy as `{ current: { data } }`. The review read the wrapper as if it were the record, and treated a missing record as "deleted".
- **Fix:** `serverCopyOf` (`src/core/utils/legacyConflict.ts`) unwraps the copy and accepts it only with a title (or an amount, for a transaction). Only an explicit `deleted: true` counts as a deletion. An unreadable copy disables the button, says why, and keeps the conflict.
- **Tests:** `legacyConflict.test.ts` and `LegacySyncReview.test.tsx`.

### S3 (P1-10). Offline start was blank
There were three causes:
1. The service worker read the PWA manifest as if it were Vite's build manifest, so no JS or CSS was precached.
2. Cached scripts missed on lookup because of `Vary: Origin`.
3. Every route waited for Clerk, which loads from another address and never finishes offline.

**Fix:**
- The build writes `precache.json`. The service worker caches each file on its own, so one missing file does not fail the install, and it matches with `ignoreVary`.
- While sign-in is checked, the screens open at once for the person whose data is on the device, but only if they were signed in when the app last ran:
  - offline, straight away;
  - on a slow line, after 4 seconds;
  - otherwise, after 0.6 seconds it says "Checking your sign-in…", or "You're offline. Sign-in will finish when you're back online."

**Tests:**
- `e2e/offline.spec.ts` builds the site, goes offline and opens three screens never visited before. It fails with the old service worker.
- `SignInWait.test.tsx` and `App.signIn.test.tsx` cover the sign-in wait.

### S4 (P0-4). On a shared device, the next person saw the previous person's tasks, budget and debts
- **Root cause:** the device had no idea whose data it held. Separation was a side effect of #89's server sync, which never succeeds against the Worker.
- **Fix** (`src/core/account/`): the device records which account its data belongs to, independently of any server.
  - When a different account signs in, the gate sets the previous account's data aside and brings back the new account's own data, if it has any. It does this before any screen or sync starts, then reloads.
  - Nothing is deleted. When the first person signs in again, their data returns exactly as it was.
  - Device settings stay put: theme, language, motion and contrast, the clock, analytics device ids, and Clerk's own state.
  - Every other key counts as personal, so a future feature is covered without being listed.
- **Related changes:**
  - The sync engine keeps its outbox and versions in one database per account, so one person's unsent changes are never sent with the next person's sign-in.
  - The old queue and its conflicts carry their account too.
  - Other open tabs reload when the account switches.
  - If the device has no room to set the data aside, nothing changes, the data stays hidden, and a notice offers "Sign out".
- **Tests:** `deviceOwner.test.ts`, `accountGate.test.ts`, `AccountDataGate.test.tsx`, `App.signIn.test.tsx`, plus the storage and `SyncRoot` per-account tests.

### S5. Found while merging #89 into the sync PR (#111)
- Two sync systems would have run side by side. #89's whole-device saving now stays off wherever account sync is on (`WholeDeviceSaving`), so the Worker build never calls `/api/state`. Settings › Your data drops its second status line there.
- The web database had two storage listeners. #89's listener re-read the store first and so swallowed the "data changed" signal that the screens and sync rely on. One listener remains. `expo-sqlite.test.ts` caught it.

## 5. Trade-offs, and what is left

**Trade-offs taken:**
- Someone who uses two accounts in one browser on purpose no longer sees one shared set of data. For financial data that is the safer default.
- Set-aside data stays in this browser's storage. Another account cannot see it through the app, but it is not encrypted.
- Offline, the app opens the last signed-in person's data without checking sign-in again. It never does so after a sign-out.
- The brand purple is slightly darker in light mode to keep AA on tinted cards.
- Changes the server permanently refuses stay on the device only. Before, they were retried for ever.
- The sync engine's "this device's data belongs to another account" choice can no longer happen, because each account has its own database. It stays as a safety net.

**Not done here:**
- "Remove my data from this device" on sign-out.
- The `$12/month` upgrade prompt, the Pro upsell while billing is off, and the public internal deployment guide. These are commercial red team P1-1 and P1-8, owned by the Accounts thread.
- Routines and rewards behind the paywall (owner's decision).
- Google Fonts are not cached, so offline text falls back to system fonts.
- The draft locales (de, fr, hi and others) were not reviewed for overclaims. Only English and es-PE ship.
- Safari and Firefox were not tested (Chromium only). There is no real Clerk sign-in in the e2e suite; the authed router is covered by `App.signIn.test.tsx` with Clerk stood in.
- React Native Paper prints a development warning.
